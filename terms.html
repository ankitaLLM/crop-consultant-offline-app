TerraSync Terms of Use

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This public release is a demonstration application using fictional grower, field, product, pricing, and agronomic information.

Permitted use

You may use this release to evaluate offline field workflows. Information remains in the current browser unless cloud synchronization is configured and you sign in. When signed in, observations and documents are synchronized to the configured Supabase project.

Agronomic and commercial decisions

Sample recommendations, rates, labels, prices, crop diagnostics, and estimates are not professional advice or confirmed offers. Verify all field conditions, product labels, legal requirements, inventory, pricing, and approvals through authoritative sources before acting.

Maps and third-party services

When configured, the online map uses Google Maps Platform and is also subject to the Google Terms of Service. The offline field view shows saved application geometry without a basemap.

Availability

This prototype is provided for evaluation without a guarantee of continuous availability, data recovery, or suitability for production use. Cloud synchronization depends on the configured Supabase project and network availability.
