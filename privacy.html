TerraSync Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This public demonstration contains fictional grower and agronomic records. Do not enter real confidential grower information into the public demo.

Browser storage

Drafts and observations are stored locally in IndexedDB in the browser you use. This release has no TerraSync backend, account system, cloud backup, analytics integration, or automatic email delivery. Clearing browser data can remove locally saved work.

Device location

TerraSync requests browser location only after you choose a location action. A location may be displayed on the map or attached to an observation when the attachment control is selected. This release stores attached locations locally and does not transmit them to a TerraSync server.

Google Maps

If a Google Maps API key is configured, the browser contacts Google to load the online map. Google may process technical and location-related information under the Google Privacy Policy. TerraSync does not cache Google map content for offline use.

Backups

The backup action downloads local drafts and observations to your device. You are responsible for protecting and deleting downloaded backup files.
