Map availability

Online: Google Maps is preconfigured for this prototype. Use its Roadmap, Satellite, or Hybrid controls directly on the map.

Offline: TerraSync shows its own saved field map—field boundaries, labels, observations, GPS position, and tracks. Google and OpenStreetMap imagery cannot be downloaded for offline use under their provider rules.

Select Save field map offline before leaving coverage. This saves all seven demonstration fields and the application files on this device.

TerraSync cloud account

Continue with the same Google account on Chrome, Edge, Safari, or another device to access the same observations and documents.

Google shares only your basic identity (name, email, and profile image). TerraSync never receives your Google password or access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendars.

Private browsing: cloud-confirmed records remain available, but unsynchronized offline work can be erased when the final private window closes.
